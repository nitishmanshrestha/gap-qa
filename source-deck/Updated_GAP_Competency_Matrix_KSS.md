## GAP is an evidence review, not an exam

## What GAP is

- A structured technical review of the growth you have already demonstrated

- Judged on evidence — artifacts, outcomes, numbers

- Role-relative: measured against the next level, not against your peers

- A conversation — the Q&A carries as much weight as the deck

## What GAP is not

- A memory test on tools, definitions or theory

- A reward for time served in the role

- A checklist needing one separate trophy per competency

- A surprise — the matrix is published, so nothing here is hidden

COVERAGE, NOT COLLECTION

## You do not need one achievement per competency

Five to six well-chosen artifacts will cover the whole matrix between them. Depth on a few beats a thin line for each.


## Start from your artifacts, not from the matrix

## Inventory

List everything you shipped this cycle — plans, suites, tools, docs, sessions you ran, incidents you handled. Do not filter yet.

## Select

Pick the five or six with the most real impact. Prefer work you can point at over work you can only describe.

## Tag

For each one, mark the competencies it evidences — one primary, the rest secondary. Then check the matrix for holes.

## Quantify

Attach a before and after number to each. Then attach the proof: the link, the dashboard, the PR, the screenshot.

MAKING IMPACT LEGIBLE

## Come with numbers, and show before versus after

"I improved the regression suite" is an opinion. "Regression went from 14 hours to 3 hours, so we moved to twice-weekly releases" is evidence. Find your baseline, then your delta, then the consequence.

Illustrative figures. If you have no baseline, say so and give the closest honest estimate with your method — a panel accepts that; it does not accept an invented number.

## Project-level recommendation

- Your project raises and endorses the case.

- Based on day-to-day performance your leads have observed directly.

- This is the gate you influence continuously, not in one presentation.

## GAP panel Recommendation

- A jury is selected from outside your project.

- You present your case, then face open Q&A.

- They know your work only through what you show them — so context matters.

- The panel gives the verdict.

## Team Manager review

- Team Managers review the outcome.

- They hold power and can stop a promotion the panel passed.

- Consistency, conduct and readiness for the wider role all weigh here.
